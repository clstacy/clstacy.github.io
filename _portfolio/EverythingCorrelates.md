A browser toy with a point. 198 breast cancer patients from a public cohort, 3,000 genes and one weather station; a median split, a Kaplan-Meier plot and a log-rank p-value for whatever you choose, plus buttons to search a thousand genes, hunt for the "optimal" cutpoint, and shuffle the outcomes so nothing can matter. The p-values do not care.
